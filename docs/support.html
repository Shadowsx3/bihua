Bihua support

How can we help?

Bihua is a local, offline study app. There is no account to recover and no cloud copy of your progress.

Restore or move progress

On iOS, open Settings → Data → Export backup, choose Save to Files, and keep the JSON file. On the other device, open Settings → Data → Import backup and choose that file. Android has its own backup export; the two formats are separate for now.

Start over

Settings → Data → Start over clears reviews, notes, and the schedule but keeps your course, theme, and pet. Uninstalling the app removes everything in its sandbox.

Reminders

Turn the reminder on in Settings → Reminders. iOS asks for notification permission at that moment. If you denied it, enable notifications for Bihua in the iOS Settings app.

Handwriting and stroke order

The first iOS build is self-graded: write on the pad, reveal the reference, and grade honestly. Characters and Practice include a stroke-order view. There is no handwriting-model download in the first iOS build.

Privacy

Read the privacy policy. The developer does not receive your notes, review history, or study list.

Contact

Open an issue at github.com/Shadowsx3/bihua/issues. Include the app version, device model, iOS or Android version, and the steps that led to the problem. Do not attach a private backup or personal notes.
