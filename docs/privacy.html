Bihua Privacy Policy

Bihua has no account, no advertising, and no developer-operated analytics. Your progress is written to a local SQLite database. This policy explains what stays on the device on Android and iOS, the few optional network actions, the permissions each platform requests, and the limited diagnostics that Google ML Kit may process.

Short version

Bihua does not sell personal data. The developer does not receive your notes, review history, or study list. Data stays in the app's local database until you delete it or uninstall the app. A full backup or an Android settings sync can include your pet choice, but only when you start it. Google ML Kit processes limited diagnostics and usage data as a service provider, as described below. iOS has no local sync and no crash attachment. The iOS widgets read a small on-device snapshot from the app group; nothing leaves the device.

What is stored on the device

  • The chosen built-in pet avatar. Bihua does not ask for a name and does not open the photo library.
  • Study settings: course, scheduler, theme, writing guide, practice modes, custom progress range, and notification choices.
  • Review history and scheduling state: cards, due dates, review logs (including optional grade reasons), XP, known-character levels, notes, study list, and word-practice history.
  • Optional memory hooks and the reusable palace pieces you write.
  • The downloaded handwriting model, if you choose to download it.
  • The reminder time and selected days, if study reminders are on.
  • Local crash files on Android, if a crash occurs, until you send or copy a report or the app prunes older files.
  • Backups and PDFs you export, wherever you choose to save them.
  • On iOS, the small widget snapshot (course, due and new counts, the featured word, and a short week summary) in the app group shared with the widget extension.

Bihua does not create an account. The developer does not receive this local data. Data remains on the device until you delete it from the app, remove the relevant item, or uninstall the app.

Android

Android cloud backup and device-to-device app-data transfer are disabled in the app manifest, so the operating system does not copy the app's database, notes, profile, or downloaded models as part of its own backup or migration. Bihua turns that off on purpose: it keeps your study data out of a Google backup account. Use Settings → Data → Export when you want a backup you control.

iOS

App data is written to the app's Application Support directory and excluded from iCloud backup, so the same promise holds on iOS. File sharing is off, so the app's data is not browsable in the Files app. Bihua has no CloudKit, iCloud sync, push, or sign-in. The small widget snapshot is not study history and is never sent to Bihua; if iCloud Backup is on, the system may include the app group container in a device backup. Use Settings → Data → Export and the system Files sheet when you want a backup you control.

A full backup or an Android settings sync includes the pet choice when you have set it, because the pet is part of the settings. A legacy photo path from a 1.1.x settings file may remain in the JSON, but current builds never read or display it.

Network access

Bihua does not run a developer-operated server. Network access is used only for these actions:

  • Handwriting model download. ML Kit Digital Ink downloads the recognition model for the active script. The model runs on the device. The download uses HTTPS, may use mobile data, is about 20 MB per script, and is optional: the app stays usable without it and still gives local shape and stroke-order feedback. On Android the download is handled by Google Play services; on iOS it is handled by the Google ML Kit pod.
  • Local Wi-Fi sync, Android only. Two Android devices on the same network can exchange a backup over a short-lived TLS connection. While the sync screen is open, Bihua advertises a temporary service through Android's network service discovery so the other device can find it; the advertisement stops when sync ends. The sender shows a 10-character pairing code derived from the session certificate. The receiver types the code and pins that certificate. The transfer is direct between the devices and is not relayed through the internet. iOS has no local sync and never asks for local network access.
  • Read-aloud. Bihua uses the text-to-speech engine installed on your device. Depending on the engine and the voices you have downloaded, that engine's provider may process the text under its own privacy policy. Bihua does not send read-aloud text to a Bihua server.
  • Links you tap. Source, licence, privacy, rating, and help links open your browser, Google Play, or the App Store. Those sites and services have their own privacy policies. Bihua does not load web pages inside the app. The App Store rating row sends nothing until you tap it.

Handwriting model downloads use HTTPS. Android local sync uses TLS with a per-session certificate pinned by the pairing code, so another device on the network cannot read or impersonate the transfer. Bihua does not send study history to any Bihua server.

Version note: Bihua 1.0.4 used a short-lived HTTP connection for local sync. Bihua 1.0.5 replaced it with TLS and certificate pinning. Current Android builds keep that TLS design.

Permissions

Android

Bihua requests only the Android permissions it needs:

  • INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE for the handwriting model download, local Wi-Fi sync, and offline status.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS for optional study reminders. The permission is requested only when you turn reminders on; reminders are off by default.
  • WAKE_LOCK, RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED, and FOREGROUND_SERVICE come from Android WorkManager, which runs the one-shot reminder job and restores it after a reboot. Bihua does not start a long-running foreground service.

Bihua does not request storage, camera, microphone, contacts, location, SMS, call log, calendar, body sensors, activity recognition, exact alarm, overlay, accessibility, device-admin, package-query, or all-files access. It does not request the advertising ID permission. The avatar is drawn by the app; no photo picker or photo-library permission is used. Newer Android versions may show a system prompt for local-network access, which Bihua uses only while the sync screen is open.

iOS

  • Notifications: optional study reminders only; the system prompt appears when you turn them on.
  • Files: access only through the system document picker, when you import or export a file.
  • Speech: the system speech voices, used only for read-aloud.

Bihua does not request camera, microphone, contacts, location, photo library, tracking, or local network permission on iOS. It does not show an App Tracking Transparency prompt because it does not track. The app has no account, no purchase, no chat, and no user-generated public content.

Third-party SDKs and AI

Bihua includes Google ML Kit Digital Ink for handwriting candidates. Your handwriting strokes and the recognition candidates are processed on the device. Bihua does not send the ink itself, your notes, your review history, or your study list to Google.

ML Kit may contact Google servers from time to time to receive bug fixes, updated models, and hardware-accelerator compatibility information. It also sends metrics about its performance and utilisation to Google. According to Google's ML Kit data disclosure, this can include device information (manufacturer, model, OS version, ML accelerators), app package name and version, per-installation identifiers, performance metrics, configured recognition languages, API configuration, feature input and output size, feature version, event type, and error codes. ML Kit encrypts this data in transit with HTTPS and does not transfer it to third parties. Google processes it as a service provider for the SDK; Google's own privacy policy also applies.

Links: ML Kit data disclosure, ML Kit terms, and the Google Privacy Policy.

Bihua does not include a separate analytics SDK, a crash-reporting service, or an advertising SDK. ML Kit is the only third-party SDK that processes data for its own diagnostics. The app does not include an advertising ID permission and does not use the advertising identifier on either platform.

For Google Play Data safety, the diagnostics above are declared as collected by the app through the SDK and not shared. For the App Store privacy labels, the same diagnostics are declared under App info and performance, not linked to identity and not used for tracking. Bihua itself has no server and receives none of that diagnostic data.

Sharing, sale, and retention

Bihua does not sell, rent, or trade personal data. It does not share data for advertising. The developer does not receive your notes, review history, or study data. A backup leaves the app only when you choose a destination in the system file picker. An Android settings sync sends only the parts you select directly to the other device on your local network; the developer does not receive that transfer. A full backup or settings sync can include your pet choice when you have set it. Google ML Kit receives the diagnostic data described above for SDK diagnostics and usage analytics; Google does not receive your notes or study history through Bihua.

Local data is retained until you delete it, clear it through Settings → Data → Start over, or uninstall the app. Downloaded models remain in app storage until you uninstall the app or clear its storage. Android crash files remain until you send or copy a report, or the app prunes old files. Backups you exported remain wherever you saved them until you delete them. If you send a report to the developer, the developer keeps it only as long as needed to read and respond, then deletes it when practical. Bihua has no user accounts, so there is no server account to delete.

Backups

A full backup is created only when you choose Export. It contains the settings JSON, the local progress database, and the pet choice when you have set it. Downloaded models and local crash files are not included. The Android file picker or the iOS Files sheet chooses the destination; Bihua does not upload the file. You control where it goes and who can open it.

Bihua does not encrypt the exported zip. Treat it like any other file that contains your study history and profile: save it somewhere you trust and delete it when you no longer need it.

Reports and PDFs

You can export a progress report as a PDF from Progress, and a practice sheet from Study tools. Bihua builds both on the device. The progress report can include the selected range, review and lesson totals, level progress, and the characters in that range. The practice sheet includes only the characters you choose. On Android the system file picker chooses where each file goes; on iOS the Files sheet or the share sheet does. Bihua does not upload either one.

Crash reports and feedback

On Android, a crash can be saved locally in app-private storage. It stays on the device until you include it in a report from Settings → About and tap Send or Copy. The composer shows the app version, build type, course, day-start hour, device manufacturer and model, Android release and SDK, locale, timestamp, thread, your words, and the crash trace if you include it. Send opens Android's share sheet so you choose the app; Copy puts the same text on the clipboard. Nothing is sent automatically. The local crash file is deleted after a successful send or copy. Crash files are not part of a backup or a settings sync.

On iOS, the report composer ships without crash-file attachment. It prints the iOS version and device model, the course, your words, and the chosen kind, never an Android release or SDK. Send opens the system share sheet. The developer receives only the text you chose to include.

Study reminders

Study reminders are off by default. If you turn them on, the time and selected days are stored on the device. At that time Bihua checks for due reviews or a new lesson and posts a local notification with a count, such as “3 characters are waiting”. The notification does not include your notes or review history, but the system may show it on the lock screen unless you change that in system settings. The developer does not receive the schedule or the notification. You can turn reminders off in Settings → Reminders, in Android Settings, or in iOS Settings.

Security

Local data sits in Bihua's app-private storage inside the platform sandbox. Model downloads use HTTPS. Android local sync uses TLS with a per-session certificate and a pairing code, and the traffic never passes through a Bihua server. iOS has no local sync. Bihua does not operate a server that could hold your study data. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so keep your device locked and protect any backup you export.

Children and age

Bihua is a general study tool, not a children's app. It is intended for users aged 16 and over, and it is not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If you are under the age of digital consent in your country, use Bihua only with a parent or guardian. Mature dictionary examples are off by default and require a confirmation before they appear. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us; there is no account to delete, and clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it removes the local data.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete individual data from the app where available, or use Settings → Data → Start over to clear cards, reviews, XP, and confusions. Notes, study lists, hooks, reasons, and settings stay until you remove them.
  • Choose a different pet or clear the name in Settings → Profile.
  • Uninstall the app to remove its local database, downloaded models, and local crash files.
  • Turn off read-aloud, sound effects, notifications, and local sync (Android) at any time.
  • Delete exported backups wherever you saved them.

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing, and the right to data portability. You can withdraw consent at any time for actions that rely on it, such as sending a report or starting a sync. Because Bihua has no account, most data is already under your control on your device. For data you sent to the developer, contact us to request access or deletion. You also have the right to complain to your local data-protection authority.

Legal bases

Where data-protection law requires a legal basis, the developer relies on its legitimate interests in providing and securing a functioning app, and on your consent or explicit request for optional actions: downloading a handwriting model, starting a local sync, exporting a backup, or sending a report. ML Kit's diagnostics are processed by Google under Google's terms as a service provider; the developer does not receive them. Bihua does not use your data for automated decisions, profiling, or advertising.

International transfers

Google may process ML Kit diagnostics in the United States and other countries under its own safeguards and terms. If you send a report to the developer, the email or messaging provider you choose may process it in another country. Android local sync is direct between your devices, and a backup goes only to the destination you choose. Bihua does not transfer your study data internationally on its own.

California privacy

Bihua does not sell personal information and does not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. It does not knowingly collect personal information from consumers under 16. The app has no account; you can delete local data in Settings or by uninstalling. For a request about a report you sent to the developer, contact us using the details below.

Changes to this policy

This policy describes the current Android app and the iOS app. If a future version adds a new network service, data source, or SDK, this page and both store declarations (Google Play Data safety and App Store App Privacy) will be updated before that release. The “Last updated” date at the top shows the current version.
