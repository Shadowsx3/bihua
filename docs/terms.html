Third-party components and the handwriting model

Bihua uses Google ML Kit Digital Ink for handwriting recognition. The ink you write and the recognition candidates are processed on your device. The app does not send your ink, notes, review history, or study list to Google.

If you use handwriting recognition, the app may download the recognition model for the active script. The download uses HTTPS, is about 20 MB per script, may use mobile data, and is optional: the app remains usable without it and still gives local shape and stroke-order feedback. ML Kit may also contact Google for bug fixes, updated models, and performance diagnostics, as described in the privacy policy. ML Kit is subject to Google's ML Kit terms and the Google Privacy Policy.
