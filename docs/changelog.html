Changelog

Small releases, kept honest.

What changed in each Bihua release, in the order it changed. No marketing fog: the work, the fixes, and the next thing.

Current release 1.0.9 1.1.0 in development 1.0.8 previous Android 10+ · offline
1.1.0
In development · 2026-11-24
next
  • Spell quest. An optional extra-practice road in all four courses: 1,387 spells across 10 chapters, including Arcana and Spirit. Every scene can be answered by a fitting family, a whole chapter, or one exact shape, and met pages are collected in the book. Nothing is graded or scheduled.
  • Meaning checks. Optional character-to-meaning reviews ask you to type the sense, then reveal a clean gloss, a real word, and your saved synonyms. Structural dictionary redirects are filtered out before they can become quiz answers.
  • Synonym shelf. Add your own wordings to a meaning and remove them later, from the reveal or the character page. They stay with the character and travel in backups.
  • Anki-style review. Optional writing, pronunciation, and meaning reviews show the cue, then Show answer, then grade. No canvas, typing, or choices. The switches are independent and off by default; scheduling and XP are unchanged.
  • Large text. The Home Study tools card, Compare header, and Paper check header keep readable titles at 1.5x instead of squeezing into vertical strips.
  • Navigation and loading. System back closes one overlay at a time, word queues no longer exit the app, model downloads and recognition have limits, and the first read-aloud tap is queued.
  • Small fixes. Clean canvas after Practise again or a target change, unique long list names, BOM-safe imports, widget count parity, and safer export errors.
1.0.9
Current release · 2026-11-23
live
  • Stylus and finger. Stylus + finger is the default, with a stylus-only choice in onboarding and Settings. A stylus down wins over a palm or finger.
  • Readable ink. Light-pressure strokes are thicker, and a tap records a dot instead of a lost stroke.
  • Word of the Day audio. The speaker now speaks the word, and the first tap waits for the voice instead of being dropped.
  • Small-screen polish. Onboarding pages scroll; paper, word practice, and Compare can return to character details; the Compare writing drill has one canvas and Off / Faint / Strokes guides.
1.0.8
Previous release · 2026-11-23
  • Stroke coaching. The exact repair stroke is highlighted, with Show me, Trace it, and Write alone. A clean repair closes with the stroke in mint.
  • Kana that reads like kana. Hiragana and katakana use a rounder, lighter guide; Han characters keep the filled brush.
  • Collapsible character sections. The 3,100-entry grid groups by level, with counts and search that flattens the list.
  • Widgets and a curated daily word. Today and Word of the Day widgets change with the hour; the daily word is always non-mature and drawn from a large quality pool.
  • Pinyin choice. Tone marks are the default; numbers are optional. Typing accepts ni3, nǐ, or plain letters either way.
  • Full feedback in practice. The handwriting check, exact repair stroke, repair modes, shape evidence, and mint closure now appear in free practice too.
  • Scheduler choice. FSRS-7 remains the default, and SBD is a second, seven-parameter model fitted from your own reviews. Both keep a memory state on every card, so switching does not reset progress.
  • Target recall. Choose 70–97% in Settings. 90% is recommended. The choice applies to FSRS-7 and SBD.
  • Calmer colour. A waiting stroke is butter and a recognition difference is sky blue; red is no longer used for practice evidence.
  • Paper check and Compare. Reveal a character and show stroke order on paper check; return to the character detail from paper, word practice, and Compare. The Compare writing drill uses one canvas size and the Off / Faint / Strokes guide row.
  • Shorter onboarding. One schedule choice, target recall, and writing as the default; pronunciation practice stays off until enabled.
  • Study lists. Named collections of characters and words, with presets, TXT/CSV/TSV/JSON import, .txt export, and a quick-add list. Lists never change the review queue; word lists open free word practice with a queue position and a Next word button.
1.0.7
Previous release · 2026-11-23

  • Filled brush guides. Real tapered outlines, numbered starts, a paper halo, and a calmer stroke check.
  • The paper-ink pet. A new launcher icon, cold-start welcome, and the warm hand-off into Home.
